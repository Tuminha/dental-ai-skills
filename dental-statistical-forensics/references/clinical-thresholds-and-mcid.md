# Clinical Thresholds And MCID

Use thresholds carefully. Many dental outcomes have contextual clinical thresholds rather than universally validated MCIDs.

## Threshold Policy

- Prefer validated MCIDs or guideline-defined thresholds.
- If no validated MCID exists, use clinically plausible thresholds only as contextual benchmarks.
- Always label contextual thresholds as contextual, not universal.
- Compare effect magnitude to measurement error when measurement error is reported or inferable.
- Do not present a threshold as definitive unless it is validated for the exact outcome and population.

## Domain Examples With Caveats

| Domain | Threshold / interpretation issue | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Ridge preservation / esthetic zone | 1-2 mm facial/buccal change can be clinically important for esthetic planning | Depends on baseline facial plate, phenotype, implant position, restorative plan, and planned augmentation |
| Marginal bone level | Small differences may be statistically detectable but clinically uncertain | Interpret with follow-up duration, radiographic standardization, and measurement error |
| Periodontal PD/CAL | Mean PD/CAL changes matter, but pocket closure and BOP often better reflect clinical usefulness | Site-level clustering and maintenance adherence strongly affect interpretation |
| Implant outcomes | Survival alone is insufficient for success | Biological/technical complications, marginal bone loss, peri-implantitis, and time under loading matter |
| Sinus lift / grafting | Vertical gain and graft percentage are not enough alone | Residual bone height, complications, vital vs non-vital graft, implant success, and loading follow-up matter |
| Diagnostic accuracy | Sensitivity/specificity require CI; PPV/NPV depend on prevalence | Thresholds and reference standard must be prespecified |
| Digital accuracy | Clinical relevance depends on span length, prosthetic tolerance, in-vivo/in-vitro setting | RMS/color maps can hide localized clinically relevant errors |

## Do Not Overclaim Thresholds

If the threshold is not established for the exact domain, write:

> Clinical threshold not firmly established for this exact outcome; this benchmark is used for contextual interpretation only.

Use thresholds to structure uncertainty, not to manufacture certainty.
