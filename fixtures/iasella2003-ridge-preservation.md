## Prompt

Audit the numerical interpretation in a ridge-preservation study comparing extraction alone with ridge preservation using FDBA plus collagen membrane.

Reported values:

| Outcome | Ridge preservation | Extraction alone |
|---|---:|---:|
| Horizontal ridge-width change | -1.2 +/- 0.9 mm | -2.6 +/- 2.3 mm |
| Mid-buccal vertical change | +1.3 +/- 2.0 mm | -0.9 +/- 1.6 mm |
| Mid-buccal vertical range | -2.0 to +4.5 mm | -4.0 to +1.0 mm |
| Final ridge width | 8.0 +/- 1.4 mm | 6.4 +/- 2.2 mm |

The authors emphasize that ridge preservation produced more predictable maintenance of the ridge, especially relevant to esthetic-zone planning.

Questions:

1. Do the mean values support an average benefit?
2. Do the SDs and ranges support the claim of individual-site predictability?
3. What should a clinician conclude for esthetic-zone treatment planning?
