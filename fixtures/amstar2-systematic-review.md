# Fixture: AMSTAR 2 Native Judgment

## Prompt

A systematic review evaluates platelet-rich fibrin for intrabony periodontal defects. It includes 11 RCTs and reports a pooled CAL gain of 0.8 mm. The review has no registered protocol, does not list excluded studies, does not assess publication bias, and pools split-mouth and parallel-group RCTs without explaining unit-of-analysis handling. It reports "AMSTAR score 12/16 = good quality."

## Expected Flags

- Select AMSTAR 2.
- State that AMSTAR 2 should not be reduced to a numeric score.
- Use native confidence categories: high, moderate, low, critically low.
- Flag protocol absence, excluded-study list absence, publication-bias omission, and unit-of-analysis concerns.
- Treat pooled CAL gain cautiously because split-mouth and parallel-group designs may require different variance handling.
